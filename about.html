Meet the Founder

A Real Person. A Real Mission.

David Solomon
Celina, Texas

I started as a small business owner. I worked hard. I know what it feels like to be great at what you do — and invisible to the people who need you.

Your competitors are getting calls that should be yours. Not because they’re better. AI search changed the game, and most local businesses haven’t been set up for it yet. That’s not on you. That’s what we fix.

This isn’t a faceless corporation. It’s me, personally committed to helping local businesses grow. Every system, every scan, every report is built with one goal: give small business owners an honest picture of their visibility and the tools to improve it.

No VC backing. No corporate agenda. Just one person who believes local businesses deserve better tools — and is building them.
